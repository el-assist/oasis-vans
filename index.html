Behind the bench

George Lewis, cabinetmaker turned van builder.

[Placeholder bio — swap in the real story.] After [X] years building kitchens and joinery on the Sunshine Coast, I converted my own HiAce for a season of chasing surf up the coast. It came back with a list of things I'd do differently, and the next one was for a friend. Word got around.

Oasis is still a one-bench operation in Noosa, and that's on purpose. I take on a handful of builds at a time so every van gets drawn up properly, dry-fitted, and finished by the same pair of hands. You'll deal with me from the first sketch to the day you drive it away — and after, when you want a shelf added or a second battery in.

Plus Olly, workshop dog and quality control.

What we do

Take the whole build or just the part you're stuck on. Any van, any size — HiAce, Transit, Sprinter, Crafter, Vito, or a bus if you're brave.

01 Full custom fit outs Empty shell to holiday-ready. Drawn to your travel style, not a catalogue layout.
02 Modular & removable kits Weekend camper, weekday work van. Bolts out in twenty minutes.
03 Electrical & solar Lithium, DC-DC, roof solar, labelled and documented so you can service it anywhere.
04 Insulation & panelling Quiet, dry, and warm. Done properly before anything gets screwed to it.
05 Windows & vents Cut, sealed and flashed. Fans and hatches sized to the space.
06 Water & plumbing Fresh and grey tanks, sinks, outdoor showers, hot water if you want it.
07 Repairs & upgrades Inherited someone else's build? Happy to fix, finish or improve it.
08 Design consults Building it yourself? Two hours with the plans will save you months.
