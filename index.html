About Oasis

Crafted by George Lewis

With over 10 years of hands-on experience, George brings a rare combination of craftsmanship and design sensibility to every van build. Based in Noosaville on Queensland's Sunshine Coast, Oasis Van Fit Outs specialises in creating bespoke van interiors that feel like home.

From full kitchens with custom cabinetry to clever sleeping platforms and hidden storage, each build is designed around how you actually live and travel. No two vans are the same — because no two adventures are.
