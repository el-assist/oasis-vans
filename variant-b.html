About
Crafted for the road, designed for living

George Lewis has spent over a decade converting vans into homes that move. What began as building his own rig for surf trips along the coast turned into a full-time pursuit of craft.

Every Oasis build is a one-off. White shiplap paneling, butcher block countertops, brass tapware, herringbone floors — the same premium finishes you'd expect in a coastal home, engineered to handle life on the road.

Based in Noosaville, George works directly with each client from concept to handover. No production line, no shortcuts — just considered design and honest workmanship.
